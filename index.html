About us

Every fabric begins as a thread and a steady rhythm on the loom.

Rooted in craft, made with intention.

We are a family handloom business with a love for Ikkat cotton. Each pattern comes alive slowly as yarn is tied, dyed and woven by skilled hands. The subtle shifts in colour and line are part of the beauty of a fabric made by people.

We choose each piece with care and are happy to help you find the right design. Browse the collection, pick the ones you love and message us on WhatsApp. We will share availability and answer your questions.
